Club platform for a junior soccer club

Community sport · Agency-side BA · Website, admin portal and mobile app

The problem
A local club for under-14s, with 65 players, 6 teams and only volunteers to run it, managed everything with a spreadsheet, bank transfers and WhatsApp groups. A fifth of fees were unpaid at round 1, and no one could confirm at a glance that every coach had a current Working With Children Check.
What I did
I gathered requirements from the committee, coaches and parents, then worked with my company's product manager, architect, designer, developers and testers. Together we are delivering a public website, a committee admin portal, and one app with separate views for parents, coaches and players.
The trade-off I resolved
Coaches wanted group chat with older players, but the child-safe policy doesn't allow it. We agreed that messages go to parents only and that players get a view-only profile their parent controls. These rules are now built into the product.

How I work

The same five stages, whatever the industry. Every requirement traces back to an objective and forward to a test, so nothing gets built that nobody needs.

  1. Discover

    Talk to the people involved and the customers affected, then agree what success looks like.

    Interviews, surveys, workshops

  2. Analyse

    Map how things work today, find the root causes and put a dollar value on each one.

    Process maps, SQL, root cause analysis

  3. Specify

    Write requirements the team can build and test, prioritised against value.

    BRDs, user stories, acceptance criteria

  4. Deliver

    Work with the team through build and UAT, triage defects and get sign-off.

    UAT plans, defect triage, go/no-go

  5. Measure

    Check the benefits actually landed, and watch the guardrails.

    KPI tracking, A/B tests, retros
