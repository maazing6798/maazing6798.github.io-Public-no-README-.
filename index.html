I gathered requirements from the committee, coaches and parents, then worked with my company's designers and developers on a website, a committee portal and an app with separate views for parents, coaches and players.

Approach

How I work

Pick a step to see what I do, and how it played out on the soccer club project.

Listen

Interviews, workshops and surveys with the people who live with the problem, so we agree on what success looks like before anyone builds anything.

On the soccer projectInterviews with 8 people, a survey answered by 38 families, and a Saturday morning spent watching match day at the ground.

Analyse

Map how things work today, dig into the data and find the real causes rather than the loudest complaints.

On the soccer projectFamilies with more than one child paid 13 days later, because the sibling discount was worked out by hand.

Define

Requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria clear enough for developers to build and testers to test.

On the soccer project28 requirements and 19 user stories, each traced to a test, including child-safety rules agreed with the club.

Deliver

Work with the team every sprint, answer questions fast, run user testing and handle changes without drama.

On the soccer projectFortnightly demos at the clubroom on training nights, and user testing with volunteer parents.

Measure

Check that the results actually landed, and keep an eye on anything that could go wrong.

On the soccer projectTargets of 95% of fees paid by round 1 and volunteer admin cut from 220 to 80 hours, with zero child-safety breaches.
